# The Five Arts — what's coming to FABLEWOOD combat (1 page, for the author)

*Full binding spec: `docs/design/magic-combat-depth.md`. Nothing below weakens the deal you approved: Strike stays free, power is bought by answering, and long-term growth is bought by your Leitner boxes. Review is about to become mana — literally.*

## The magic system

**Your five actions become five schools — the Five Arts** — and each Art grows to three tiers:

| Art | You cast it by… | Tier 1 (today) | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kindling | multiple choice | Smite | Cinder-Rebuke (hit + shove) | Lanternfall (burst + burn) |
| Binding | matching pairs | Binding Weave | Snare-Knot (roots them) | Lattice of True Names |
| Ritecraft | ordering | Ritual Sequence | Processional (sweeps the line) | Rite of Sealing (marks them) |
| Attestation | free recall (the Clerk) | Cast From Memory | Attested Ward (a sworn shield) | Verbatim Decree (8 dmg) |
| Courtcraft | scenario choice | Parley | Heartening Word (rally) | Injunction (a stopping writ) |

- **Learning spells = welding facts.** Keeper's Rank 2 (all tier-2 spells) opens at 8 facts in box 3+; Rank 3 at 8 facts in box 4+. Your due-queue grind IS the XP bar, and the HUD shows the carrot: `5/8 welded toward Rank III`.
- **Glow** — a little 3-pip lantern meter each battle. Strikes that land, standing on lanterns, lighting braziers… and **renewing a DUE fact with a perfect answer** each feed it. Tier 2 costs 1, tier 3 costs 2 — *on top of* the question, never instead of it. Tier 1 stays free-to-cast forever, so questions are never rationed.
- **Weld edge:** a perfect cast on a fact you've kept at box 4+ hits +1 — *"the welded truth strikes true."* Mastery pays out continuously, and you can't farm it (the due queue decides what you're asked).
- Wellspring drills now leave a **Trimmed Wick**: your next battle starts with 1 Glow. Morning coffee reviews power the evening crawl.

## The BG3-ification

- **Eight conditions** with honest counters: Kindled (burning), Bound (rooted — constructs are immune), Dimmed, Warded, Inked (takes +1), Emboldened, Weakened, Staggered. Chips under HP bars, hover to read.
- **Positioning that bites:** flanking (+1 for melee pincers — theirs too), opportunity strikes when anyone retreats sloppily, **Careful Step** (half move, no provoke), and **pushes** — slam a wraith into a wall for bonus damage. Two new tiles: **braziers** you light mid-fight (Glow + grace) and **mist** that blocks ranged lanes for both sides.
- **Enemy kits:** wisps hunt flanks in pairs, wraiths dim your legs and kite away, constructs slam you across the board and can't be rooted.
- **The Vault-Warden, reforged:** tougher (12 HP), pushes you around, roots your best unit with Crown Injunction, summons a clause-wisp — and his **"Objection!"** overrules the first free Strike against him every round. He can never overrule an answered question. Knowledge always lands.

## Your retinue

At the South Gate you now choose who rides: **Mira the Factor** (her Appraise inks a target so everything hits it harder; at high mastery she tallies bonus gold off marked kills) or **Alder the Gatewarden** (slow, unkillable-adjacent — Hold the Gate wards the line and draws fire). Both scale with your mastery of the province — and both visibly dim if you neglect it.

## Rollout (each phase playable on its own)

1. **The living board** — conditions, kits, flanking, pushes, new tiles. Fights get dangerous first.
2. **The Five Arts & Glow** — ranks, tier-2 spells, the flyout, glow items in Mira's shop.
3. **Apex & the Warden** — tier-3 spells, the reforged boss fight.
4. **Retinue & ritual** — Alder, the gate choice, the Trimmed Wick.

**Three calls you can veto** (defaults chosen, nothing blocks phase 1): enemies get opportunity strikes too (default yes — teeth-but-fair); party stays Keeper + one chosen companion rather than growing to three (default yes — protects every save); Rank 2 needs 8 welded facts (say the word and it's 6).
